01 The shift

Search didn't die. It got an upgrade — and a gatekeeper.

AI engines now answer your customers directly, citing a handful of sources and ignoring everyone else. Classic SEO gets you ranked on a page fewer people visit. GEO gets you inside the answer.

/ 01

The old funnel is shrinking

Roughly 69% of Google searches now end without a single click. When an AI summary appears, clicks on classic results drop by half. The blue link is no longer where decisions happen.

/ 02

Winners take almost everything

AI answers cite about five sources — and the top domains capture most of those citations. Meanwhile, roughly a quarter of brands never get mentioned by AI at all. Absence is invisibility.

/ 03

Early movers compound

Engines learn from consistent, structured, fresh signals. Every citation you earn today makes the next one more likely — and makes you harder to displace tomorrow. The moat is built now.

07 FAQ

Questions we get asked. Fittingly.

What exactly is generative engine optimization? +
GEO is the practice of engineering your content, site structure, and off-site authority so AI engines — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Grok — cite, quote, and recommend your brand when they answer the questions your customers ask. You'll also hear the same discipline called AEO — answer engine optimization. Different label, same goal: instead of competing for position #1 on a results page, you compete for a place inside the answer itself.
Is GEO replacing SEO? +
It builds on it. Clean technical SEO is still the foundation — engines can't cite what they can't crawl. But ranking is no longer the finish line: the majority of searches now end without a click, and AI-referred visitors convert at multiples of classic organic traffic. We treat SEO as the floor and GEO as the game.
Which engines do you optimize for? +
ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, and Meta AI — plus the crawlers and indexes that feed them. Each engine sources differently (their citation overlap is surprisingly small), so we track and optimize them separately rather than pretending one strategy fits all.
How long until we see results? +
Faster than classic SEO, honestly measured. Generative engines heavily favor fresh content — most-cited pages skew to those updated within 30 days — so early citation movement typically shows within weeks of shipping. Meaningful share-of-voice shifts against competitors usually take two to four months of consistent iteration. We report both from day one.
How do you measure AI visibility? +
Structured prompt panels run against each engine on a schedule, tracking whether you're mentioned, cited, or recommended — and with what sentiment — across the question space that matters to you. We tie that to AI-referred sessions and conversions in your analytics, so visibility connects to revenue, not vanity.
Do you also design and build websites? +
Yes. We design new websites and rebuild existing ones so the foundation itself is GEO/AEO-ready — clean structure, fast loads, and schema baked in from the first line of code. A recent build is msinterio.in, the complete website for MS Interio, an interior design studio in Jaipur.
What does the free audit include? +
A baseline of where you appear across the major engines today, the questions where competitors get cited and you don't, the technical and content gaps behind that, and a prioritized 90-day plan. It's real work, delivered as a document you keep — whether or not you hire us to execute it.
