How much does GEO cost in India?

Pricing6 min read·Updated 2026-08-31
The short answer

GEO engagements in India generally fall into three bands: a one-off audit (roughly ₹25,000–₹75,000), a monthly retainer (roughly ₹40,000–₹2,00,000+ depending on scope and competitiveness), and project work like a GEO-ready website rebuild (roughly ₹75,000–₹4,00,000). What moves you within a band is the number of engines and prompts tracked, how much content is produced, and whether off-site authority work is included.

Why nobody publishes prices

Most agencies won't state numbers, which leaves buyers unable to judge whether a quote is reasonable. We think that's a bad trade for a discipline that's supposed to be about answering questions honestly, so here are real ranges — with the caveat that any specific engagement depends on scope.

The three commercial shapes

1. Audit and roadmap — ₹25,000 to ₹75,000

A one-off diagnostic. You should receive: a prompt-panel baseline across the major engines, a technical assessment of crawlability, rendering and structured data, an entity and off-site audit, a competitor comparison, and a prioritized plan. It should be useful whether or not you hire the agency to execute it.

Below about ₹25,000, you're likely getting an automated report rather than analysis. Above ₹75,000 for an audit alone, ask what's included that isn't on this list.

2. Ongoing retainer — ₹40,000 to ₹2,00,000+ per month

The main engagement model, because GEO compounds and one-off fixes decay. Typical bands:

3. Project work — ₹75,000 to ₹4,00,000

A GEO-ready website build or rebuild, priced by site complexity. The distinction from an ordinary web project is that structure, schema and answer-first content architecture are designed in rather than retrofitted — which is substantially cheaper than building twice.

What actually drives the number

  • Competitiveness. Displacing entrenched national players takes far more sustained work than establishing a local business as a canonical answer.
  • Content volume. The largest variable cost. Genuinely good, sourced content is not cheap to produce and is the main engine of results.
  • Engines and prompt count. Six engines × 50 prompts weekly is real labour, whether manual or tooled.
  • Off-site work. Digital PR and authority building are the most expensive component — and, per the mention-vs-backlink data, the most correlated with outcomes.
  • Starting position. A site with clean technical foundations costs less to move than one needing a rebuild first.

How to tell real GEO from relabelled SEO

The industry has a rebranding problem. Ask these five questions before signing anything:

  1. "How will you measure AI visibility?" A real answer describes a prompt panel with per-engine tracking. A vague answer about "AI-optimized content" is a red flag.
  2. "Show me a baseline report." If they can't show the format of a starting measurement, they aren't measuring.
  3. "Which engines, tracked separately?" Blended numbers hide the fact that engines overlap far less than assumed.
  4. "What's your off-site plan?" If everything happens on your own domain, they're skipping the part that correlates most with results.
  5. "What do you guarantee?" The correct answer is process and reporting — not rankings or citations. Anyone guaranteeing a ChatGPT recommendation is either misunderstanding the mechanism or misrepresenting it.

Frequently asked

Is GEO cheaper than SEO? +
Comparable at similar scope, and often faster to show movement because engines weight freshness heavily. The cost structure differs: less spend on link acquisition, more on content quality, measurement and digital PR.
Can a small business afford GEO? +
Yes, particularly for local markets. The highest-impact local actions — a complete Google Business Profile, consistent details across platforms, a review process — cost nothing but time. Paid help becomes worthwhile once those are done and you're competing on content and authority.
Should I pay for a guaranteed ChatGPT recommendation? +
No. Nobody controls what an AI engine recommends, and there is no paid placement in organic AI answers. A guarantee is a sign the seller either doesn't understand the mechanism or is willing to misrepresent it.
How long before I should expect results? +
Early citation movement typically appears within weeks of shipping answer-ready content. Meaningful share-of-voice change against established competitors usually takes two to four months. Judge an agency on whether they're reporting a real baseline and honest movement, not on speed alone.
